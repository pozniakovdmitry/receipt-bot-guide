01

First, get set up.

If the bot asks you to register, follow these prompts in order.

Step 1

Send /start

Open your Telegram chat with Receipt Bot and send /start.

Step 2

Enter your full name

When the bot asks for your Full Name, send your real name, for example John Smith.

Step 3

Choose Sales

After saving your name, the bot asks for your role. Tap 👔 Sales.

Step 4

Wait for approval

The bot tells you your request is pending. You will receive a separate message when it is approved.

Your Employee Code is assigned by the administrator and included in the approval message. You do not enter it during this registration flow. Start sending receipts once approved.
02

Send first. The bot sorts next.

You do not need to choose a category before uploading.

1

Send a photo or PDF

Send it straight into the chat, or tap 📸 Add Receipt first. Use a clear photo or a PDF containing readable text. If a scanned PDF cannot be read, send a photo instead.

2

Let the bot read the receipt

It extracts the details and suggests the category. If it cannot determine the category, it asks you to choose one.

3

Answer any questions

For a restaurant or café receipt, Sales users are asked whether the meal was with a client. For journeys in GBP, confirm one way or return, then confirm or enter the route. Add a comment if prompted.

4

Check the category first, then the details

The category is prominent near the top of the review screen. If it is wrong, tap 🔄 Change Category. Check the vendor, date and amounts; for overseas receipts, check the currency too.

5

Tap ✅ Confirm and Save

Wait for the success message with your receipt ID, then send the next receipt. The success wording varies.

Uploading does not save the expense. Finish the questions and press Confirm and Save. If you have an unfinished receipt, the bot offers ⬅️ Back to my receipt or 🗑 Discard it before you start another.
03

Check where the receipt landed.

These are the nine category options available to Sales.

“Was this meal with a client?”

For a restaurant or café receipt, tap 👥 Yes — with a client or 🙋 No — regular food. Your answer determines Client Entertainment or Food. This question also applies to restaurant receipts in a foreign currency.

The manual category list is a fallback when detection is uncertain, or when you tap 🔄 Change Category. You do not need to upload the receipt again to change its category.

Food

01

Food and drinks bought in shops, or a restaurant/café meal you confirm was not with a client.

Over £35 on one receipt? Explain why in a comment.

Parking

02

Parking receipts in GBP, including car parks, meters and parking apps.

Parking in a detected foreign currency goes under Business Trip (Abroad).

Hotel / Accommodation

03

Accommodation receipts in GBP, including hotels, B&Bs, guest houses and Airbnb.

Taxis, buses and similar transport use the journey flow below.

Ticket(One way)

04

A journey in GBP you identify as one way. This includes taxis, Uber, Bolt and buses as well as tickets.

Confirm or enter the route when prompted.

Ticket(Return)

05

A journey in GBP you identify as a return trip. The bot asks you rather than relying on the ticket wording.

Confirm or enter the route when prompted.

Client Entertainment

06

A restaurant or café expense in GBP you confirm was with a client. Check this category for other client entertainment too.

A comment is required: who was there and what it was for.

Vehicle Expense

07

Fuel or EV charging. For Sales, a receipt containing both fuel and food is also assigned here.

A petrol-station receipt containing only food belongs in Food.

Other Expenses

08

Choose this when a business expense does not fit another category. If detection is uncertain, the bot asks you to choose rather than automatically assigning Other Expenses.

Business Trip (Abroad)

09

A detected non-GBP currency takes the receipt into the overseas flow. The bot uses the receipt type to suggest the subcategory, asking you if needed.

04

For GBP journeys, answer two checks.

Train and flight tickets, taxis, Uber, Bolt, buses and similar transport.

1. One way or return?

After reading the receipt, the bot asks “Was this journey one way or return?”

Tap ➡️ One way or 🔁 Return. The bot asks this even if the ticket already contains journey-type wording.

2. Is the route correct?

If a route is detected, check it and tap ✅ Yes, correct or ✏️ No, enter it myself.

London → Manchester

If no route is detected, the bot asks you to type it as From → To. You can also use ✏️ Edit Route on the review screen.

05

A receipt in another currency?

The bot detects non-GBP receipts and starts the overseas flow automatically.

Food goes to the overseas Food subcategory. Travel, accommodation, parking and fuel go to Travel-Accommodation. For restaurant or café receipts, your answer to the client question decides between Food and Client entertainment.

FoodTravel-AccommodationClient entertainment
  • Check both the detected category and currency before confirming.
  • Keep the amounts in the currency printed on the receipt; do not convert them to pounds.
  • If the bot cannot determine the subcategory, it asks you to select it.
  • For overseas Client entertainment, add a comment explaining who and what it was for.
The automatic overseas decision is based on the detected currency. If the category is wrong, use 🔄 Change Category. The ordinary Food category's £35 comment rule does not apply to overseas Food.
06

A quick check makes the difference.

Check the category, then correct any details that look wrong before saving.

Need to change a detail?

Tap ✏️ Edit Manually. Choose Vendor, Date, Net, VAT or Gross, then send the corrected value.

Repeat as needed. Tap ✅ Done, review the result and confirm to save.

Use DD/MM/YYYY for dates and a decimal point for amounts, for example 12.50.

Seeing ⚠️ CHECK?

The recognised amounts may not add up. Compare them with the receipt and correct any errors before confirming. If the figures match the receipt but the warning remains, ask for help rather than changing correct figures.

Net is the amount before VAT; VAT is the tax shown; Gross is the total.

The bot can make mistakes. Always check the receipt, even when no warning appears.
07

A little context, when it matters.

The bot asks for a comment in these situations.

Comment required

Client entertainment

For UK Client Entertainment and the overseas Client entertainment subcategory, explain who the expense was for and its business purpose.

ExampleLunch with Alex from the client team to discuss the upcoming project.
Comment required

Food over £35

For the ordinary Food category, explain why a single receipt exceeds £35. You must add the comment before the bot will save it.

ExampleDinner during a late return from a customer visit; limited options near the station.
For other receipts, use 💬 Comment (optional) to add helpful context. Once added, tap 💬 Comment ✓ to update it before saving.
08

Help, without the hunt.

A few useful commands to keep close.

/startShow the main menu
/helpView quick help and your categories
/mystatsSee your count and last five receipts
/cancelCancel the current receipt
The bot cannot read my receipt. What next?

Choose Try again and send a clearer photo or file. Keep the receipt flat and well lit, with the date and total visible. If a scanned PDF cannot be read, send a photo instead.

I picked the wrong category.

On the review screen, tap 🔄 Change Category and select the correct category. For Business Trip (Abroad), select the subcategory too. You can keep the current category if you change your mind; no new upload is needed.

The bot says a receipt is still waiting.

It has not been saved yet. Tap ⬅️ Back to my receipt, finish any questions and confirm to save. Choose 🗑 Discard it only if you do not want to save that receipt. The bot may also remind you after a few minutes of inactivity.

Where can I find the longer guide?

Tap 📖 Instructions in the main menu or send /instructions to receive the PDF guide.
